Founded on Excellence

Reviving the Grandeur of Continental Dining

At Clinton Restaurant, Continental cuisine is not merely an assortment of recipes — it is a revered cultural dialogue between Europe's greatest culinary empires.

Conceived over a decade ago in the heart of Manhattan by Chef Clinton Vance, the restaurant was built to restore the ceremonial splendor, table-side finesse, and technical mastery of legendary European establishments.

Here, classical French veloutés and slow wine reductions merge seamlessly with the fragrant simplicity of the Mediterranean, the rich braises of Central Europe, and the delicate pastry architecture of Vienna. Every meal is an unhurried, multi-sensory celebration of flavor and hospitality.

Gastronomic Philosophy

The Three Pillars of Clinton Gastronomy

Our culinary ethos is guided by an uncompromising commitment to authenticity, craftsmanship, and pure sensory harmony.

Technique & Discipline

Strict brigade de cuisine execution following the classic Auguste Escoffier codex, elevated with modern thermal precision and artistic plating.

Terroir & Pure Sourcing

We source exclusively from heritage farmers and coastal fisheries: Périgord truffles, Brittany butter, Wagyu cuts, and seasonal wild mushrooms.

Art of Hospitality

Intimate table-side carving, decanting of rare vintages by certified sommeliers, and personalized degustation adjustments for every esteemed guest.

Master Behind The Repertoire

Executive Chef Clinton Vance

Grand Maître de Cuisine • Alumnus of Institut Paul Bocuse, Lyon

With over twenty-five years of experience across three-star kitchens in Paris, Milan, and Vienna, Chef Clinton brings rare classical erudition and visionary mastery to every dish.

"A magnificent dish is an emotional time capsule. It carries the fragrance of Normandy pastures, the coastal breezes of the Mediterranean, and the warmth of a candlelit European salon."

His philosophy celebrates the purity of every cut and harvest. In his kitchen, shortcuts do not exist; veal reductions simmer for forty-eight hours, and each pasta sheet is hand-kneaded to gossamer translucence.
