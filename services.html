Frequently Asked Questions

We maintain an elegant and sophisticated atmosphere. The dress code is Business Elegant or Formal. Gentlemen are encouraged to wear collared shirts and jackets; athletic wear, baseball caps, distressed denim, and beach footwear are strictly prohibited.
Our kitchen brigade is well-versed in accommodating gluten intolerance, dairy sensitivities, nut allergies, vegetarian, pescatarian, and halal diets. Please notify us at least 24 hours prior to your seating so that Chef Clinton can tailor your degustation seamlessly.
For standard dining room tables of up to four guests, cancellations may be made with 24 hours advance notice without penalty. For parties of five or more, private salon buyouts, and Chef's Table reservations, a 48-hour notice is respectfully required.
Guests are welcome to bring up to two 750ml bottles of rare personal vintage wine that are not currently featured on our 2,800-bottle list. A corkage fee of $95 per bottle applies, which covers expert cellar temperature chilling, sommelier decanting, and Riedel Sommeliers Series stemware.
Complimentary white-glove valet parking is provided directly at our main entrance on 742 Grand Boulevard for all dining guests during both lunch and dinner service.
