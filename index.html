The Essence of Clinton

Where Continental Heritage Meets Haute Gastronomy

Founded upon centuries of European culinary traditions, Clinton Restaurant reinvents classical recipes through contemporary precision, rare terroir sourcing, and unyielding dedication to hospitality.

Michelin-Trained Artistry

Under the direction of Executive Chef Clinton Vance, every plate balances classical French discipline, Mediterranean vibrancy, and meticulous modernist plating.

Artisan & Terroir Sourcing

Direct partnerships with independent organic farmers, Brittany oyster harvesters, Piedmont truffle hunters, and alpine dairy artisans ensure pristine ingredient integrity.

The 2,800-Bottle Cellar

A subterranean sanctuary holding rare Premier Grand Cru Classé Bordeaux, Barolo riservas, and vintage champagnes curated by our renowned Master Sommelier.
